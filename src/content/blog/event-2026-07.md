*Women In Tech: Imagine Construction Reshuffled by AI & Corporate to Entrepreneur*

This HKWD session spotlighted women in technology with two talks on AI in construction and the path from corporate career to independent entrepreneurship.

## Talk 1: (Dare to) Imagine Construction, Reshuffled by AI

Construction is a trillion-dollar industry still heavily driven by PDFs, relationships, and gut feel. This talk explored how AI is reshaping the ecosystem, from tender writing to building design.

### About Adeline Chan
*CEO and Co-Founder, AAL INNOVATION*

Adeline Chan is an architect-turned-entrepreneur and AI transformation leader focused on architecture, engineering, construction, and real estate. She shared practical lessons from building her consultancy and creative studio at the intersection of construction and technology.

## Talk 2: Leaving Corporate to Become a Self-Made Tech Entrepreneur

Kerrie shared her journey from corporate employment to entrepreneurship, including projects she is building and practical ways independent developers can use AI tools, APIs, and technical docs to create business solutions.

### About Kerrie Mariah
*Software Engineer and Tech Consultant*

Kerrie Mariah is a software engineer and consultant with experience in web development, system automation, and API integrations, focused on building practical and scalable applications.
