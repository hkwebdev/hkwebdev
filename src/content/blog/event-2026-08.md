*Teaching Taste to an Agent*

This HKWD session focused on teaching taste to an AI agent, with a talk on designing systems that can reason across culture, operations, and enterprise governance.

## Speaker: Augustin Chan

Augustin Chan is the CTO and founder of Digital Rain Technologies. His work includes agent evaluation and enterprise AI safety-focused projects, and he co-hosts the Hong Kong AI Podcast.
