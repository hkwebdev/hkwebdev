*Remote Work & Harnessing The Beast*

Two talks this month — one on carving out a borderless career as an AI product designer in Hong Kong, and one on taming the chaos of AI-assisted development with a deterministic harness.

## Talk 1: Crafting an Unconventional Path — Remote Work and Freelancing as an AI Product Designer in Hong Kong

The conventional design career path is shifting. Over the past five years, Evian has navigated the digital nomad lifestyle, evolving from full-time remote roles to launching her own independent practice in Hong Kong. She shares proven strategies for remote work and designing a life on your own terms, plus a look at her portfolio merging refined visual aesthetics with cutting-edge tech — visual identities, art direction, and product design for the AI space.

### About Evian
*UX/UI Designer*

Evian is a UX/UI designer making waves in China and Hong Kong, specialising in owning remote work post-COVID.

## Talk 2: Harnessing the AI Beast!

Using a deterministic harness for undeterministic development. A practical look at how to bring structure, repeatability, and confidence to AI-assisted software projects — so you can move fast without losing control.

### About Daniel Sarosi
*CEO, Great Wall Connect*

Daniel Sarosi is the CEO of Great Wall Connect, leading the team with a vision for innovation and excellence. With 20+ years of experience working with software and cloud technologies, he is passionate about helping businesses succeed in the digital landscape.
