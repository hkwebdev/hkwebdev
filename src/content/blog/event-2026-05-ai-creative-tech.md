*AI And Creative Tech*

This session of HKWD focuses on creative tech and AI, bringing together leading practitioners who push the boundaries of what's possible at the intersection of engineering, design, and brand storytelling.

## About Ilmari Heikkinen
*Award-winning Creative Technologist, SXSW 2025 Innovation Award Winner*

Ilmari is an award-winning Creative Technologist and SXSW 2025 Innovation Award winner with 20+ years of experience bridging the gap between high-performance engineering and visionary brand storytelling. A pioneer in web standards (WebGL) and an expert in Generative AI, he has spent the last decade as a high-level consultant for world-class agencies and in-house teams. He specialises in delivering "world-first" digital experiences for iconic brands including LVMH, Chanel, Google, and HSBC.

## About Rhys Turner
*Co-founder, Creative Technologist and Director, Tronica*

Rhys Turner is a Creative Technologist and Innovation Technologist, specialising in the intersection of technology, design, and luxury experiences. With a background in emerging technologies, AI, and interactive media, he explores innovative ways to enhance digital storytelling and customer engagement. His work focuses on blending creativity with technical innovation, pushing the boundaries of digital craftsmanship in the fashion and beauty industries.
